F (User Experience) — Policy III.F.2a
YouTube logos and icons must follow the Branding Guidelines
(minimum icon height 20 dp; shape and corners unmodified)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

RESOLVED.

1) Size

The icon flagged in your screenshot is the Long-form / Shorts toggle on our
Hot-Tube tab. Measured in production today:

    Tab bar                              34 x 24 px
    Long-form / Shorts toggle (flagged)  34 x 24 px
    In-app guide list                    34 x 24 px

    Minimum required: 20 dp      Measured: 24 px  (+20% margin)

These are not container sizes. We measured the red YouTube artwork itself by
scanning the rendered screenshot for contiguous #FF0000 pixels and taking the
bounding box — the same quantity your report measures. Figure 1 in the attached
PDF shows the measurement drawn on the live screen.

The 20 dp remediation was deployed on 2026-08-18 09:26 KST and covered all
eight icon locations, including the toggle shown in your screenshot. We have
now raised the height to 24 px so that rounding or measurement tolerance
cannot bring it below the 20 dp minimum.

2) Shape and corners

The icon is the unmodified official artwork (the two official paths,
#FF0000 and #FFFFFF). On the live page the icon element computes to:

    border-radius: 0px      transform: none      clip-path: none
    filter: none            opacity: 1

The rounded corners visible around the icon in your screenshot belong to the
button that contains it (border-radius: 10px, overflow: visible), not to the
icon. No radius, transform or clipping is applied to the YouTube icon itself.

3) How to verify

Open https://galla.im/search?tab=hot and run in the browser console:

    document.querySelector('.hv-mode-btn .yt-official').getBoundingClientRect()

Height returns 24.

We remain committed to full compliance and are happy to provide any further
detail you need.
